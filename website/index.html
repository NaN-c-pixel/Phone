Connect to your HùngOS

The control interface is served by your ESP32-S3 — this page is the project’s public info page.

Power on

Start the ESP32-S3 and let it connect to your configured 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.

Find its address

Read the local IP address printed in the MicroPython serial/REPL output.

Open the interface

On a device connected to the same network, open http:// followed by the board’s local IP address.

🔒 The board interface is intended for your local network. This public page does not connect to or expose your ESP32.
